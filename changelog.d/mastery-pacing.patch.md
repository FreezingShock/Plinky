Pads level up faster and matter more: a pad's yield grows 0.8% a level (+20% at level 25, was +2.5%), and its XP cost grows 1.25 times a level, so a pad reaches level 25 in about 7 hours of pressing. The Larkmoor gate opens at about 75 minutes of active play (was about 106), and idle play takes about twice as long as active.
