The Ember Quarry has its own ascension, the Nucleus. The forge collapses to its core and pays Core Shards, which buy 13 forge-themed gifts (start Embers, Stoked Furnace, Shard Veins, Forge Hands, Banked Rows and more), with its own centred animation. Rebirth, Prestige and the old generic Upgrades are retired; old quarry Rebirths and upgrade levels convert to Core Shards when a save loads.
