The meadow path is bolder and easier to read: a wider path with a dark rim and pebbles on its surface, flower verges along both edges, lantern posts that light the path, low timber rail fences on the gate approach, and a plank boardwalk out to the lake. The meadow gate's vine and drifting petals are larger, the foam on the stream's banks is wider, and the gate's hover area covers the whole arch.
