Larkmoor has more room under the node ceiling. Props now load in smaller 160-texel chunks, so the most a walker can see at once is about 160 props instead of about 300. The walker still always has at least 320 texels of props loaded around it. Textures and materials are shared per file, so a chunk that loads or unloads no longer makes new copies, which trims the hitch when the walker crosses into a new area.
