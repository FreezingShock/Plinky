The zone 1 meadow is the 3D render below (`world/meadow3d_scene.tscn`, loaded by main.gd). The old baked 2D backdrop and gen_meadow occluders were removed.
Composition rules: clustered props, y-sorted overlap, off-grid positions, noisy island edge, shade pools under trees, path wanders. Light stays upper-left, so lit props are not flipped.

## meadow3d/ (3D-rendered meadow)
Generated by `python3 tools/art/gen_meadow3d.py` (numpy + Pillow). Real 3D heightfield terrain and SDF-voxel props, orthographic 2:1 iso, lit and shadowed, then 3-band Bayer dither to the zone 1 palette with per-object ink outlines.
- `ground.png`: 840x480 with 100 px (left/right) and 60 px (top/bottom) of bleed around the 640x360 frame (mountains and water), so wide windows can extend the view. Place it at (-100,-60); all anchors and sprite coordinates stay in the 640x360 frame (sprite x/y may be negative). Terrain, water, plinth and ground cover only (cast shadows from trees are baked in).
- `sprites/*.png` + `placements3d.json`: trees, big rocks, crystals and bushes as separate sprites with a `foot` anchor for y-sorting. The sprite is the pixels of that object visible in the full scene.
- `placements3d.json` `anchors`: pad, gate, shrine and player spawn positions in texel space (nothing baked, the game draws them).
- `preview_full.png`: the composed scene for reference only.
Scene: `world/meadow3d_scene.tscn`. It adds the Ground sprite, `Anchors` (Pad0..4, Gate, Walker, Shrine, Water_lake, Water_pond) and `spawn_occluders(parent)` for the y-sorted sprites.
