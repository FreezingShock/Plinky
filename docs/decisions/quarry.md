# Decisions: Ember Quarry (zone 2)

The quarry: zone 2 blockout, map, forge ladder, Embers, Cinder, Reforge, and the zone 2 balance. Index: [README](README.md). Append new decisions to the end of this file as `## <date>: <title>` with one line of reasoning.

## Zone 2 blockout (23 Nov)
- Zone scene, walk mask and heat numbers are data on ZoneDef (`scene_path`, `walk_mask_path`, `heat_*`). main.gd swaps the zone scene on entry. WalkMask has `use(path)` for the active zone.
- Forge buttons cost Cash, not Rebirths, since Rebirths cannot be earned in zone 2. f1/f2/f3 = 1e4/1e5/1e6 for +1/+8/+64 Slag. Provisional.
- Slag is a StatDef with `income_factor_per_unit = 0.5`, a generic extra income factor. It does not reset on rebirth. The HUD shows its row only past zone 1.
- Heat is charged by standing on vents (anchors Vent0..), not by presses. Hot press pays 2x. Heat is not saved. Sim has `feed_heat`, `is_hot`, `heat_hot_changed`; the world node HeatVents feeds it.
- Zone 2 gate (Slag 9) has no next zone yet, so walking in does nothing and the HUD says nothing is past it.
- Placeholder quarry tint on the meadow removed; zone 2 has its own palette-index art.

## 2026-11-23: Ember Quarry fixes (trees, gate, cliff placement)
- Meadow trees in the quarry were Larkmoor props that survived the zone swap: `main.gd` freed old props by the `Occ_` name prefix, but a new sprite with the same name as one still queued for deletion is auto-renamed `@Sprite2D@n`, so it escaped the next cleanup. Props are now tracked and freed by reference (`_spawn_occluders` / `_free_occluders`); `tests/test_quarry.gd` covers it.
- The quarry gate is drawn in code (`TexelGate`, `ZoneDef.gate_art = "forge"`): basalt pillars, iron bands, ember seams, a grate while shut, a gold shimmer when open, hover tooltip. Zone 3 does not exist yet, so an open gate shows the banner "The way on is still being forged" instead of travelling. Hook up `next_zone` on zone2.tres when Tidewater lands.
- Cause of the "perspective glitch": the quarry's near rim wall is raised, so the lowest tier was flat in the height map but hidden on screen behind the wall. `tools/art/gen_zone2.py` now computes which cells are visible, keeps the walk mask and props inside them, and snaps the spawn and pads to visible ground.

## Ember Quarry forge ladder (2026-11-23, v0.30.0)
- **Decision (Nathan):** the quarry gets a 6-row stat ladder like the meadow's, with custom isometric art. Rows bottom to top: **Cinder** (pays Multiplier, costs the passive Cash), **Ore** (Iron Ore), **Slag**, **Ingot** (Ingots), **Alloy** (Alloys), **Emberheart** (Emberhearts), rarities Common to Mythic. Cash stays the zone's passive trickle, Multiplier is the first row's stat. Data: `content/tiers/{cinder,ore,slagrow,ingot,alloy,emberheart}.tres` (the Slag row id is `slagrow` so it never clashes with the stat), 18 pads `<row>_1..3` (+1/+8/+64), stats in `content/stats`.
- **Buffs:** reuses `StatDef.buff_stats` / `buff_factor`: Slag 1.25x on Multiplier and Iron Ore, Ingots 1.25x on those and Slag, Alloys 1.5x, Emberhearts 1.5x, each on everything below. The `gain_boost_stat` chain still runs one step down (Iron Ore boosts Multiplier, Slag boosts Iron Ore, and so on). Rebirths no longer boost Multiplier (there are no Rebirth pads any more); the quarry's Rebirths stat is now a count that rises by 1 per rebirth.
- **Rebirth is zone scoped (balance thread's soft-lock finding):** `ZoneDef.prestige_source_stat` (quarry: Ingots, otherwise Clover Leaves), `Sim.prestige_source()`; a rebirth resets only stats live in the zone it was done in; Cash restarts at 10 and the first Cinder pad costs 10, so there is no dead end.
- **Arrival grant:** flat. With `entry_source_stat` empty, `entry_grants` pay the plain value: 190 Cash on top of the start 10. Before, it scaled with Starbloom and bought the gate in 2 s.
- **Gate:** 600 Emberhearts. `tools/sim.gd` now plays the quarry: rows open at +3, +6, +15, +26 and +48 min after arrival, gate at about +64 min (greedy floor, seeded; the balance thread tunes from here).
- **Saves:** `ladder = 2`. Old quarry saves keep Cash, Multiplier, Slag, Rebirths; Forge pad mastery f1..f3 moves to `slagrow_1..3`; rows up to Slag open, and all rows open if the old gate was already beaten.
- **Layout:** `tools/art/place_quarry_pads.py` puts 18 pads on the visible terraces (a row per terrace strip, zig-zagging up toward the gate) and rewrites the pad anchors in `placements3d.json`. Vents, gate and spawn are unchanged; two rows sit close to the spawn and road.
- **Art:** `tools/art/gen_quarry_ladder.py` renders 16^3 voxel models (coal pile, ore chunk, slag pool, ingot stack, anvil, ember heart) in 2:1 isometric with a ramp per tier colour (matching rarity), smooth tone bands (dither only at band edges), ink outline and 3 flicker frames; outputs hero, pad-size (`_game`) and 32/16/8 px icons into `assets/art/zone2/ladder/`. Pads draw the `_game` frames (`TexelPad._draw_forge`); locked rows are a dark silhouette, opening rows rise with the same spark ring as a flower.
- Not run in the cloud: real-hardware engine look. Tests (2862 checks) and the sim run headless here.

- **HUD currency panel is a compact strip (v0.30.0).** Names removed (instant tooltips carry them); value takes the stat colour; gain is 8 px dim text left of the value; rows show only once the stat has been held (latched per session) and only in the current zone. Chosen over a "headline + fold" panel because every earned currency stays glanceable and it needs no extra click. Collapse toggle left for later.

## 2026-11-23: Ember Quarry balance pass (v0.37.2)
- **Pad costs x0.6** (from the shipped values) on all 18 quarry pads (`content/buttons/cinder_*` to `emberheart_*`). Rewards unchanged. Greedy floor (no walking, momentum, relics, heat or attributes), from the arrival purse: active gate about 40 min after arrival, was about 66. Idle (0.2 presses/s, the idle profile from #174) gate about 78 min, ratio about 2.0. **The greedy floor is erratic here:** x0.58 gives active 29 min and idle 105 min (ratio 3.7), and x0.5 gives 38 and 48 (ratio 1.3). Treat the 2x ratio as a point estimate. The seeded engine sim over the profiles is what should confirm it.
- **Prestige per quarry rebirth x (1 + 0.25 x quarry rebirths so far)**, set by `ZoneDef.prestige_per_rebirth_bonus = 0.25` on zone2 only. Counted by the `reb` stat (quarry rebirths, not reset), so meadow and Larkmoor rebirths do not inflate it. Model (one attribute track, cheapest first, rebirth at Ingot 9, 6 hours, levels 1 to 15 only since the caps stop buys there): attribute gaps, median and max, were 14 and 63 min before, 4.4 and 8.1 min with this pass. Beyond level 15 the gaps still grow with the 1.4x cost; the caps (8 to 25 levels) stop that in practice.
- **Attribute growth stays 1.4x** (Nathan, via coordinator). Growth 1.25 to 1.3 would shrink the late gaps further; not needed for now.
- **Idle profile not mirrored in `tools/sim.gd`:** the profile framework (`content/config/sim_profiles.tres`) is in #174, which is not on main yet. This port uses the same idle rate (0.2 presses/s).
- **Idle at about 2x active** (Nathan's target, via coordinator): a player who only idles reaches the same milestones in about twice the time. Checked on the greedy floor as above; not yet checked over seeds in the engine sim.
- **Rebirth in the quarry:** the arrival purse is a flat 190 Embers and the quarry resets only its own stats, so a quarry rebirth leaves a player at Embers 10 with a cinder row (cost 6 Embers now) that can restart the chain. Checked by reading the code and the port, not in engine.
- Not run in engine: the balance port is Python from the GDScript, validated against the PR's own greedy result (66 min vs about 64 min claimed). Headless tests run in CI.

## 2026-11-23 Cash and Multiplier leave the Ember Quarry (0.34.0)
- **Nathan's call:** Cash and Multiplier are removed from the quarry and will return in a later nostalgic, liminal zone. They are parked, not deleted: `content/parked/stats/cash.tres` and `mult.tres` (not loaded), the `cash.png` and `mult.png` icons, and the `CASH_MULT` and `MULT_GAIN` relic effects stay.
- **Embers** replace Cash as the quarry's passive trickle (the Sunlight counterpart): orange, `embers`, start 10, boosted by Cinder, Prestige and income relics. Chosen over Heat because Heat is already the vent mechanic, and over Kindling to keep the Ember Quarry name in play.
- **Cinder** replaces the Multiplier as the first forge stat (row 1, Common, `cinder`), so the six rows read Cinder, Ore, Slag, Ingot, Alloy, Emberheart with the same chain: Embers pay Cinder, Cinder pays Ore. All costs, rewards, the 190 Embers arrival purse and the buff rule are unchanged, so the quarry balance (#180) still applies one for one.
- **Rebirth stays in the quarry until Reforge.** It never needed the Multiplier: it banks Ingots as Prestige, Prestige multiplies Embers income and buys Attributes, and Rebirth Points buy perks. The Reforge ZoneReset (PR #183 framework) should replace it as its own follow-up, with a forge-themed currency and menu.
- **Saves:** `ladder = 3`. Saves from before it move `cash` to `embers` and `mult` to `cinder` on load (`Sim._rename_quarry_stats`). Older saves still go through the flower and quarry ladder migrations first.
- Not run in the cloud: the engine (tests and screenshots are CI and the PC tester).

## Ember Quarry rework: roomy terraced world, lava, front-facing 2D art (2026-11-23)
- **Nathan:** the starting area was far too cramped, wanted more variety, lava features that block walking, glow from the lava, and "more detailed 2D, facing the screen" art (he did not mean 3D isometric).
- **World:** the quarry is now one 1400x760 canvas like Larkmoor (`QuarryScene.world_rect`, `mask_offset` 0, ground.png and walkable.png are canvas sized). `tools/art/gen_quarry.py` replaces the quarry half of gen_zone2.py and `place_quarry_pads.py` (deleted). Six terraces rise 16 to 22 texels each from the arch to the gate; every row has about 70 texels between pads and 88 texels between rows, a switchback road past each row, and a stair ramp up each step. Flanks have mesas, craters, ash patches, ember seams and a canyon rim.
- **Lava:** two rivers (east one spills down all five terrace steps as falls), 12 pools and a lava moat across the south end that the road crosses on a stone bridge (the way back sits beyond it). All lava, cliffs and a 3 px margin are not walkable; the generator floods the mask from the spawn and fails if any pad, vent, gate or arch cannot be reached.
- **Glow, cheap:** the warm light is baked, not computed at runtime. The ground and every prop sprite near lava have their palette indices shifted along the ember ramp (0.. rust.. orange.. gold) in up to three Bayer-dithered bands. The lava itself ships as 6 frame strips in 128 px tiles (`lava/`), cycled by `world/lava_flow.gd` at about 5 fps, only for tiles on screen, frozen under Reduce motion. Embers also rise from six pool centres (`Lava` anchors read by LifeEmbers).
- **Art direction change:** quarry sprites are drawn straight on (front-facing 2D), lit from the top left, built with `tools/art/qs.py` (faceted rock, crystals, polygons with ramp tone, rim light, ink outline). `gen_quarry_ladder.py` redraws the six pad objects (charcoal heap, ore crystals, crucible, ingot stack, anvil, heart gem) in that style, same file names and sizes class. `tools/art/qprops.py` makes 15 prop kinds (basalt columns, boulders, crystals, ore veins, ruins, braziers, stelae, dead trees, stalagmites, mine timbers, cart, iron lamps, lava rocks, posts). The old isometric voxel renders are gone.
- Capture: `godot --path . tools/quarry_shots.tscn` (hub, arch, gate, four lava spots, one pad per row).

## 2026-11-23 The Nucleus: the Ember Quarry's own reset
- **Nathan's call:** the quarry still ran the old Rebirth, Prestige and generic Upgrades. It now has its own ascension, the **Nucleus**, on the ZoneResetDef framework (`content/resets/nucleus.tres`). The forge collapses to its core, pays **Core Shards**, and every pad pays +25% for good (same pacing as Bloom: the requirement shrinks 15% per use, floor 30%). Original design: the name and mood nod to a crystal-cavern core, nothing is copied.
- **Resets** Embers, Cinder, Iron Ore, Slag, Ingot, Alloy and Emberheart. Keeps gates, open rows, relics, mastery, Core Shards and the meadow. Opens with the quarry gate (600 Emberheart), measured on Emberheart.
- **Gifts** (13, `content/attributes/nucleus_*.tres`, paid in Core Shards, opening as Nuclei are done): Warm Start (start Embers), Stoked Fire (Embers income), Anvil Plates (pad reward), Slow Burn (offline), Shard Veins (more shards), Twin Strike (double press), Ash Stride (walk), Forge Hands (auto-press), Flint Edge (crit), Banked Rows (keep the lowest rows), Core Echo (more bonus per Nucleus), Anvil Study (pad XP), Heart Strike (free strikes on the top open row). New effect ids: `ember_start`, `ember_rate`, `shard_pay`, `nucleus_bonus`, `keep_rows`; the rest reuse the generic ones.
- **Retired in every zone:** Rebirth button, Prestige and Rebirth Point rows and the Prestige-bought Upgrades (`GameConfig.rebirth_zones` is empty; the sim functions stay as parked code). Embers no longer take the Prestige multiplier. Every zone reset now counts toward the relic slots (5 and 20), as Rebirths did.
- **Saves:** `ladder = 4`. A save that has been to the quarry turns each old Rebirth into 2 Core Shards and one Nucleus use (at most 8, `nucleus_carry_max`, so the pad bonus carries on), and each level bought in the old upgrades into 1 Core Shard; Prestige, Rebirth Points and those levels are cleared. Meadow-only saves are left alone.
- **Animation:** the existing centred ritual (veil, closing ring of marks, burst, count-up, fly to the HUD row, reduced-motion respected, no shake) with a forge skin from the def: ladder colours, `.:+#` marks, particles that rise like sparks (`ZoneResetDef.fx_*`).
- Not run in the cloud: the engine. CI and the PC tester run tests and capture the animation.

## 2026-11-24 The Ember Quarry is underground: its light holds at midday
- **Nathan's call:** the quarry darkened and went blue at night like the meadow, but it is underground. The lava, rocks and heat sources should light it, and it should be bright at all times.
- **How:** `SkyCycle.underground` (set by `quarry_scene.gd`) computes every clock-driven light value at the midday model time (`SkyCycle.underground_time`, phase 0.5). That covers the shade, grade, sun, rim light, haze, mist, ground light and cast shadows. Weather and wind are off. `clock_phase()` and the HUD clock still read the real time, and the meadow is unchanged because the flag defaults to false.
- **Glow:** `lava_glow` 0.4 to 3.5, and `lava_night_boost` to 0 (the glow no longer rises at night). Each source now adds a white-hot centre (`glow_hot_gain` 1.8) and a wide soft halo (`glow_halo_reach` 2.6 times its radius, `glow_halo_gain` 1.4), both in the additive glow layer. This is a light falloff, not a screen blur, because the theme forbids blur. `prop_glow` 4.0 scales the light rocks, crystals and pillars take from the sources. Vents are the quarry's heat sources: `vent_glow` 0.9 and `vent_radius` 40. Braziers `brazier_glow` 1.2. Haze stays at `quarry_haze` 0.3 (at 0.6 the cool recesses went murky), and `shaft_strength` 0.55.
- **Check:** `tests/test_lighting.gd` `run_underground`: the real clock is dark at midnight, the held time has no night and is at midday. `tools/quarry_shots.gd` takes `phase=0.0` or `phase=0.5` to pin the sky for matched before and after shots. After the change, the midnight and noon lava shots match apart from the HUD clock and animation.
- Measured on the headless noon capture (xvfb, lavapipe): about 20% of the frame's pixels are more than 10 luma brighter, mostly around the lava river, lava rocks, braziers and lamps. Clipping is unchanged (0.58% against 0.56% before). It is warmer, not a true bloom. PC QA should judge whether it reads as glowing enough at 1x.

## 2026-11-24 The tier art seen from above, on each terrace
- **Nathan's call:** the pad art is too much of the quarry's look to lose, so the original tier sprites stay on the forge pads. Their reperspective versions go around the level, one tier per terrace.
- **How:** `tools/art/qrelief.py` turns each `assets/art/zone2/ladder/<tier>_game.png` into a height relief. A column stands as high as the sprite's silhouette in that column, takes the body colour of the sprite, and runs back six texels. It is ray-marched from the same elevated 2:1 view as the meadow beds and lit from the upper left, with a one texel ink outline. `tools/art/gen_quarry.py` places two of each tier on its terrace (`TIER_PER_ROW` 2, footprint radius `TIER_FOOT` 11). They use their own random stream and are placed after the other props, so the existing 183 props keep their positions. The reliefs are ordinary y-sorted props with normal maps (`gen_quarry_normals.py`).
- **Check:** `tests/run.gd` passes (7929 checks). An in-engine run of `tools/quarry_shots.tscn` (phase 0.5, xvfb with lavapipe) shows the reliefs on their terraces beside the pads. The walkable mask carves a footprint around each one.
- **Not done:** the reliefs have no side-wall shading yet, so their steps read as blocks. PC QA should judge the night and dusk tones and whether the two copies per terrace repeat too much.
