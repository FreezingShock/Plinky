# Decisions: Audio and ambience

The Audio autoload, footsteps, ambient soundscape, world-life sounds and press sounds. Index: [README](README.md). Append new decisions to the end of this file as `## <date>: <title>` with one line of reasoning.

## Audio (23 Nov)
- Procedural soft-synth audio, generated by tools/audio/gen_audio.py, no third-party sources (assets/audio/LICENSE.md). Chiptune vs soft synth is open for Nathan; soft synth chosen as the cosy default.
- New autoload `Audio` (audio/audio.gd), added to project.godot (integrator file; flagged in the PR). Buses Music, SFX, UI, Ambience created at runtime under Master. Volumes persist in user://audio.cfg.
- Wiring: Juice.press and Juice.relic_drop call Audio; EventBus gate_opened, rebirth_done, zone_entered, button_denied are listened to by Audio; every BaseButton gets hover and click sounds via node_added; walker calls Audio.footstep.
- Press pitch climbs the C major pentatonic ladder when presses land within 650 ms of each other.
- Loop flags are set in code, because .import files are gitignored.
- No loading screen exists yet: Audio.start_loading() and stop_loading() are ready for it.

## 2026-11-23 Quiet pad clicks
- Nathan: standing on a pad clicked forever. Settings > Audio now has "Quiet pad clicks" (on by default) and "Clicks before quiet" (slider 1 to 10, default 5). After that many presses in a row the pad click and hold tick go quiet. Stepping off or releasing a pad resets the count, as does a 1.5 s pause. Crit sounds always play. Settings are stored per id with defaults in settings.cfg, which has no schema version, so there was nothing to bump.

## 0.23.0 World life and footsteps (2026-11-23)
- **Found already built:** butterflies, dusk fireflies, pollen, seasonal petals/leaves/snow (SeasonParticles) and grass/sand/wood footsteps. This pass fills the gaps rather than redoing them.
- **Butterflies follow the calendar.** Up to 6 out at summer noon (was 2 all the time, even at night). Spring 0.8, summer 1.0, autumn 0.35, winter 0; none at night, fewer at low sun, none in heavy rain. Each has its own wing colours (accent, paper, light over earth/accent). Fireflies are a summer thing (spring 0.7, autumn 0.45, winter none). `MeadowLife.season` carries the mix, written by SkyCycle with the same weights as SeasonParticles.
- **Ember Quarry gets its own air layer** (`world/life_embers.gd`): 7 embers per vent rising and cooling (paper, glow, flame, dark), 16 drifting ash flakes. Whole-texel squares, no alpha, 12 fps, frozen under Reduce motion, thinner on Low.
- **Cinder footsteps.** New surface `cinder` (four variants, generated by `tools/audio/gen_audio.py cinder`): the quarry floor is loose rock, its pale road is packed dust (reuses `sand`), pads and the gate stay wood. The quarry scene never told main.gd which ground to read, so quarry steps were using the meadow's map; it now names its own `ground_path` and `placements_path`. Side effect: the quarry no longer plays meadow shore or birdsong by position (it has no water or trees).
- **Step weight.** `Audio.footstep(surface, extra_db)`: a sprint lands up to 2.5 dB heavier, a hard landing up to 5 dB, a jump push-off 1 dB. Crouch and slide stay silent as before.
- Not run in the cloud: the engine. CI runs the headless tests; the PC pass checks the look and the sound.

## 2026-11-24: Rain sound follows SkyCycle rain (recommendation 9, second of three)
- **Sound:** a rain bed made in code on first use (`AmbientScape._rain`): low-passed noise for the patter, a few soft ticks for drops on leaves, 6 s at 11025 Hz, mono 16-bit, with the last quarter second blended into the head so the loop does not click. No file added. It is made once, about 6 ms here.
- **Level:** `rain` sits at -16 dB on the Ambience bus, under the wind and crickets.
- **Gain:** `AmbientMix.rain_gain(rain01, snow_w, area)`, a pure rule. It follows `MeadowLife.rain`, is silent under snow (winter rain is snow) and in the quarry (no weather there). `AmbientScape` reads it on each poll, so it fades like the other layers.
- **Why:** the handoff's recommendation 9. Rain was seen but never heard.
- **Not verified by ear:** the cloud has no audio output. The level, the loop seam and the tick density need a listen on the PC.

## 2026-11-24: Sound pass 2 (dirt, wheat, purchase, lava)
- **Found already built:** crickets, day birds, wind (day, night, winter), autumn leaves, shore and pond lapping, procedural rain, grass, sand, wood and cinder steps, press and streak sounds, UI click, hover and back, and the Audio settings (Master, Music, Effects, Interface, Ambience, mute, quiet pad clicks). This pass fills the gaps only.
- **Sounds:** `step_dirt_0..3` (soft thud with grit), `step_wheat_0..3` (dry stalk brush), `sfx/purchase` (two pentatonic plinks, short enough to repeat), `amb/lava` (24 s loop of murmur and rising bubbles). All from `tools/audio/gen_audio.py dirt wheat purchase lava`, own RNG per sound, so no existing file changes.
- **Surfaces:** `MeadowSites` reads the farm's `farm_soil` and `farm_wheat` frames from `farm.json` (Larkmoor only, never the quarry). Wheat wins where the frames overlap. Frames are the field rectangles, so stalk-free margins still count as wheat.
- **Purchase:** `Audio` listens to `EventBus.attribute_bought`. Pad and upgrade buys play the same chime, with no jitter so repeats match.
- **Lava:** a second Ambience player, faded in by `set_zone` in the quarry and out elsewhere. Sits at -5 dB, under the forge crackle.
- **Why:** Nathan's sound list (audio pass, 2026-11-24). Wiring waited for his approval of the preview.
- **Not verified by ear:** the cloud has no audio output. Levels were measured with ffmpeg volumedetect. The farm frame coordinates were checked against `farm.json`, not against the walker in-engine.
